# Commercial licensing

Smart Arm Controller is released under the
[SmartArm Noncommercial License](LICENSE). That license covers a lot of ground
for free, and deliberately stops short of one thing.

## You do not need to pay

- Learning from the code, reading it, running it, taking it apart.
- Personal projects, hobby robotics, experiments, portfolio pieces.
- Research and academic work.
- **Teaching, as long as the learners are not charged for the course.** Free
  workshops, school clubs, community classes, open courseware, a YouTube
  tutorial — all fine, and welcome.
- Modifying it and sharing your modified version, under the same license.

## You do need a commercial license

- Shipping it, or anything derived from it, in a product or service you sell.
- Using it internally at a company for commercial purposes.
- **Paid instruction** — any course, class, workshop, bootcamp or training
  program that uses this software and that learners pay a fee to attend. This
  holds whoever runs it: a private coaching centre, a company, or a nonprofit.
  If learners are paying, a commercial license is required.

The line is the fee, not the institution. A university running a free open
workshop needs nothing. A private academy charging students for a robotics
course that uses this software needs a license.

A fee that only recovers the cost of materials, hardware or the venue, and
returns no surplus, is not a paid course for these purposes.

## Getting one

Open an issue on the repository, or contact the maintainer through
[github.com/Shivenderthakur](https://github.com/Shivenderthakur), with:

- who you are and what organization you represent
- what you want to use the software for
- rough scale — number of learners, seats, or units shipped per year

Terms and pricing are agreed case by case.

## If you are not sure

Ask. Describing your situation in an issue costs nothing and takes a day to
answer, and it is much cheaper than getting it wrong.
